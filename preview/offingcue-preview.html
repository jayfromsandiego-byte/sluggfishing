Mexico and boundary guidance

Do not reduce jurisdiction to one line
Mexican waters

Permit requirements follow jurisdiction, not the app label

Fishing in Mexican waters requires current Mexican authorization. Passport, FMM, vessel, and protected-area requirements depend on route and proximity to Mexico's coast or islands. Verify with CONAPESCA, INM, the Mexican Consulate, and the captain or landing.

CONAPESCA source
Map meaning

12 nm is a territorial-sea limit, not a universal “international water” switch

The map separates the U.S.–Mexico maritime boundary from the 12 nm territorial-sea line. A coastal state can retain fisheries jurisdiction beyond its territorial sea in its EEZ.
